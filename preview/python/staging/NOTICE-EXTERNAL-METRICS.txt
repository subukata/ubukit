Third-party provenance for this isolated candidate
==================================================

The public score definitions, ordered-pair ARI final expression, AMI limiting
branches, and signed epsilon-clamp compatibility expressions were developed
with scikit-learn 1.8.0's installed sklearn.metrics.cluster._supervised source
open for comparison. Treat these small compatibility portions as adapted from
scikit-learn; its BSD 3-Clause copyright/conditions/disclaimer are retained in
THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt. Upstream source file hashes are in source_audit.json.

The candidate's label encoding, bounded dense/sparse contingency selection,
within-call unordered marginal-size grouping, mode-centered normalized full
hypergeometric recurrence, batched NumPy implementation, optional Numba kernel,
and benchmark/test harnesses were written for this experiment. No Cython
implementation was copied or compiled into this candidate. The elementary
recurrence was independently derived from adjacent hypergeometric probabilities
and checked against integer-combinatorial/Decimal oracles.

No scikit-learn, NumPy, SciPy, or Numba binaries or full packages are bundled.
The dependencies retain their respective licenses. No project-wide license is
selected or changed here. This private repository snapshot is not a registry release.
