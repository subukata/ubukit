UbuKit JavaScript license scope
==============================

UbuKit's project-owned contributions are licensed under the MIT License in
LICENSE, copyright (c) 2026 Seiki Ubukata. The project-developed WebAssembly
kernels, including src/metric-wasm.js and its embedded binary, are included.
Their source-preservation and reproducible-build records are maintained in the
repository's wasm/ directory; reconstructed source remains labeled as such.

The original SOM-OLP and corresponding derived code retain LICENSE-SOM.txt.
Adapted scikit-learn compatibility expressions retain LICENSE-SCIKIT-LEARN.txt
(BSD-3-Clause); NOTICE-EXTERNAL-METRICS.txt identifies their scope. These original
notices are unchanged and are not replaced by the project MIT selection.
The package's MIT AND BSD-3-Clause expression records these combined declared
source terms; it does not relicense the BSD portions under MIT alone.

Repository-only historical kernels, rebuild tools and dataset fixtures are
outside the npm runtime file list. Historical project kernels use the project
MIT terms. Tool dependencies and dataset sources retain their separate terms
and notices if separately distributed.

No additional conditions restrict rights granted under the applicable licenses.
