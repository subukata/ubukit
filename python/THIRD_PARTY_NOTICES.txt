scikit-learn
------------

Three small expressions in the adjusted Rand index and the adjusted mutual
information follow scikit-learn's sklearn/metrics/cluster/_supervised.py
(adjusted_rand_score and adjusted_mutual_info_score), from which they were
first adapted at version 1.8.0:

- the ARI from pair counts, 2 (tp tn - fn fp) / ((tp + fn)(fn + tn) +
  (tp + fp)(fp + tn)), and 1.0 when fn = fp = 0;
- the AMI of 1.0 when each labeling has a single cluster (or there are no
  labels);
- the AMI denominator kept at least machine epsilon away from zero, with its
  sign.

They are in python/src/ubukit/metrics.py and js/src/metrics.js (ari, ami).
The rest of UbuKit's metrics, including the expected mutual information, was
written for UbuKit.
